# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want Block Forge to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for people of every experience level.

## Expected behavior
- Be kind, patient, and respectful.
- Give actionable feedback and accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for learners and the project.
- Respect differing opinions and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized or violent harassment are not acceptable.

## Enforcement
Report abusive behavior privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed on the GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary.
